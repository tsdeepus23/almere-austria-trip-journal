Day 4

The pass that felt like a finale

The last stretch climbed into the mountains, and suddenly the whole trip felt bigger than the map. The road curved gently, the air sharpened, and the view kept opening for all of us traveling together.

Into the Alps

This was the moment I think about most when I remember the drive. The road was not dramatic in a loud way, but it felt important. Like the trip had reached its quiet center.

It was the kind of place where you want to stop, breathe, and let the road speak for a while—especially after a long journey shared by two families with children, a baby, and a pregnant traveler.
