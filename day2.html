Day 2

Rain, coffee, and the river light

We stopped for coffee and short breaks just as the weather turned soft and gray. Somehow that made everything feel more intimate, like the trip had settled into itself with two families and two children in tow.

Along the Rhine

The Rhine gave the day a different rhythm. There was water on one side, hills on the other, and the sense that we were drifting into a story we had already started to love.

I remember the way the light changed every time the clouds shifted. It was never dramatic, but it felt personal, especially with the baby needing comfort and the seven-year-old girl pointing out every little thing along the way.
