Day 3

Salzburg at the edge of evening

We arrived just as the day began to soften. The lake was calm, the air was clean, and the whole place seemed to invite us to slow down together as a family.

Quiet by the water

There are evenings that stay with you because they do not ask for anything. This was one of them. We walked a little, sat a while, and let the day end without rushing it.

The best part of the trip was not the destination, but the feeling of being exactly where we needed to be—with the little one resting, the older child relaxed, and the pregnant traveler finally able to breathe more easily.
