Day 1

Leaving Almere with a full heart

The first miles felt lighter than expected. We left with snacks, little songs, and a lot of patience, traveling together with two families, a one-year-old baby, a seven-year-old girl, and a pregnant traveler.

That first stretch

I still remember the quiet of the morning as we rolled out of Almere. It was one of those mornings that felt almost too calm, like the world had paused just long enough for us to slip into it.

The road made room for our thoughts, our laughter, and our little breaks. The radio stayed low, the windows were cracked open, and every kilometer felt like a small promise shared by all of us.
